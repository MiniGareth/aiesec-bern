Conferences

What are AIESEC Conferences?

One of the greatest benefits of AIESEC membership is the opportunity to participate in our conferences. These events bring together local committees (LCs) from across Switzerland, uniting members and alumni in a shared space. Here, participants engage in sessions focused on leadership and self-development, fostering both personal and professional growth.

Beyond the educational value, AIESEC conferences are an ideal opportunity to expand your network. Connect with current members, alumni, and individuals from diverse cultural and international backgrounds. Build lasting friendships and professional relationships that extend far beyond the event.

Ask any member. This is where the real memories are made.
