Membership Experience

Being a member is more than just being in the departments. Yes, this is where you can learn, hone, and apply practical skills related to the department. This is the place where the things are created, ideas realized, and what keeps our community up and running. Learn leadership, time-management, storytelling, and other soft skills you won't get just from your studies.

As a member you are part of the circle. Be part of the hangouts we have in the evening. Join in the events, both those organized for people outside the organization, and also those directed at the members. We belive that the university memories that stay for life, are those made by doing random things with the friends around you.

Meet kind, bizzare, odd, interesting, eccentric people. A multitude of personalities and cultural background. Make friends with the people in Bern, Geneva, Lausanne, Fribourg, Saint-Gallen, Zurich, and from beyond the border. Discover what this so called larger "AIESEC Network" is.

It's really a unique environment, a place of growth, innovation, and adventure. The place is as you make it to be, what there is to gain is up to you to grasp.

Make new memories. Make new experiences. Meet new people.
