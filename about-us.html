About AIESEC

AIESEC is the world's largest youth-led organization, present in over 100 countries and territories. We develop leadership qualities in young people through international internships, volunteer exchanges, and leadership opportunities.

AIESEC in Bern is our local chapter, connecting students and young professionals in the Bern region with global exchange opportunities and a community focused on personal growth, cultural exchange, and social impact.
