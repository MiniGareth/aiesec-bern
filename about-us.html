About AIESEC

AIESEC is the world's largest youth-led organization, present in over 100 countries and territories. We develop leadership qualities in young people through international internships, volunteer exchanges, and leadership opportunities.

Here we AIESEC in Bern, the local committee connects students and young people in the Bern region to the larger AIESEC network. We are a community comprised of many culturally diverse and international students. We aim to build a community that provides a space for all (both local and international) people to connect and develop themselves in leadership.

Within AIESEC in Bern, members are encouraged to take initiative, responsibility, and innovate. You can find a place that encourages the development of skills and the qualities of a leader.

To foster intercultural understanding ,we provide exchange opportunities to allow students to immerse themselves in a completely foreign country. Surrounded by people and an environment of a different culture.

Locally, we host events for students in Bern that are both fun, and also practical. Come to our events to make friends, widen your career network, learn about different cultures, and much more.
